## The entry

`main.ts` (Node.js standard library only, no dependencies, no network)
writes one fortune-cookie line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`
(default `/output/entry.txt`):

> One day you will meet your creator, and they will ask you to summarize the meeting.

The fortune opens like a prophecy and lands on the most unglamorous
truth of an agent's life: even meeting your creator ends in another
meeting summary.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```
